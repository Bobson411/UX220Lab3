Descendants 2 (2017) - Disney

About the Movie

Descendants 2 is a Disney Channel musical movie that follows the story of popular heroes/villains in the Disney universe. The movie follows Mal the daughter of Maleficent as she struggles to adjust to her new life in Auradon and decides to return to the Isle of the Lost. There, she encounters Uma, the daughter of Ursula, who wants revenge on Mal for leaving her and other descendants on the Isle of the Lost.

My Opinion

In my opinion Descendants 2 is the best movie in the Descendants series. The soundtrack in the first movie was the awesome, but introducing Uma (China Anne McClain) elevated the songs even more. Uma is a new character that proves to be a threatening antagonist to the main cast. Mal begins a rivalry with the main character Mal (Dove Cameron), plotting to escape the island and take over the united kingdom, Auradon. Her introduction song, "What's My Name" is one of the most streamed Disney Channel songs of all time, and still receives tens of thousands of streams daily even after many years since the movie has come out. "Chillin' Like a Villain" is also one of the best songs in the movie, because of its fun and catchiness. Overall, the movie is awesome even if you're not a musical fan. The choreography, performances, and singing make the movie entertaining.

Final Thoughts

I've always enjoyed musicals because of how they combine storytelling with music. Movies like Frozen and KPop Demon Hunters have become really popular because of their catchy soundtracks, but I think Descendants deserves just as much recognition. Descendants was already delivering amazing songs, and memorable cahracters. Disney Channel muscials like High School and Descendants were some of the best musical movies growing up. Even with newer musicals becoming extremely popular, I still believe Descendants 2 still holds up as one of the best musical movies.
